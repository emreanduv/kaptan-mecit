**Full description**
Kaptan Mecit is a football game that feels like a real match on your phone: play 11-a-side in a 3D stadium or pick the top-down, retro Classic view. 28 leagues on four continents, continental cups and an Intercontinental Cup, a league career and squads updated every week.

The ball stays at your feet; change direction sharply to feint past a defender, rise for headers from high crosses, plant your foot and pick your corner. Keepers dive on time and hold on to the ball they catch. When you score the stands erupt and your scorer runs to the corner flag.

• Modern: 3D stadium, broadcast camera, night / sunset / day matches, match report and goal replays
• Classic: top-down pixel-art retro football
• 28 leagues, 496 clubs; continental cups for Europe, the Americas, Asia and Africa, plus an Intercontinental Cup
• League mode: table, fixtures, top scorers, squad, awards, statistics, history
• Edit mode: rename clubs and players, change colours and shirt numbers, share your pack with friends
• Squads updated every week
• 34 formations, playing style (defensive / balanced / attacking), team talks, substitutions
• Fouls, cards, penalties, offside, VAR, defensive walls at set pieces
• Turkish and English; a Guide and a How to Play screen
• No ads, no data collection

All club and player names are fictional.

Made by Mr. Anduv!

Headers in Modern: players leap for high crosses. Skill moves: change direction sharply in front of a defender to feint past him and keep the ball. Shoulder-to-shoulder battles, player faces and five hairstyles. Kick-off time: night, sunset or day. Edit mode and squads updated every week.

**What's new (1.7.0)**
Edit mode is here: rename clubs, colours, players and shirt numbers your way; share your pack with friends or load theirs (same pack in Classic and Modern). Squads are now updated every week. In Modern the ball stays at your feet, players plant before shooting, the camera is closer and keepers hold the ball they catch.

**What's new (1.4.0)**
Built on our testers' suggestions: a Guide with short tips the first time you meet each screen (turn it off any time) and a How to Play screen from the main menu, the pause menu and Settings. Share your wins and titles with friends, rate the game in the store, and send feedback from Settings with Write to us. Delete my data removes your career and settings from the device. Thanks to our test team!

**What's new (1.3.0)**
Squads now come from the Champion Battle Manager world: 6 leagues, 114 clubs and familiar-sounding fictional player names. Pick from 34 formations before kick-off or let your assistant choose. Fouls, advantage, yellow and red cards, in-match penalties, offside and VAR. Slow-motion goal replays. Players jog to the ball for set pieces, and the referee never stops play during a dangerous attack.

**What's new (1.2.0)**
Substitutions are here: a 7-player bench, 5 substitutions in up to 3 windows (half-time doesn't count). Let your assistant make the changes if you like, based on fatigue, injuries and the score. Players tire as they run and can get injured. The referee shows stoppage time and never blows the final whistle in the middle of an attack. Pitchside boards feature Mr. Anduv! social and music accounts.

**What's new (1.1.0)**
Five camera views including a TV broadcast angle. Teams now play a real 4-2-3-1: overlapping full-backs, build-up from the keeper and centre-backs, a pressing holding midfielder, a No.10 between the lines, crosses and headers, counter-attacks and a defensive line that moves with the ball. Teams switch ends at half-time, keepers are stronger, and a new steep, close-to-the-pitch stadium with a roof, executive boxes and rotating LED pitchside boards.
