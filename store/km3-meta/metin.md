## Long description (EN)
Kaptan Mecit 3 is a football game played in a packed stadium under the floodlights. Watch and play the match from the touchline, from the TV camera position or from a miniature pitch on the table in front of you; just turn your head from your seat.

• 6 leagues, 114 clubs, 34 formations and an assistant manager who picks the best one for your squad
• Running, sprinting and kicking taken from real human motion capture; goalkeepers dive, fall and get back up
• Fouls, advantage, yellow and red cards, penalties, offside and VAR
• Players tire and get injured: check their fitness at the break and make your own substitutions, or leave it to the assistant
• Every stand supports its own team: the scoring side's fans erupt and chant, fouls get whistled, cards get booed, and the sound comes from where the stand is
• 6 seats: tabletop model, touchline, TV, dynamic, tele and stand; smooth fade between seats and the camera never moves on its own
• Tips that appear in your first match when you need them, and a How to play screen any time
• No ads, no purchases, no data collected, no internet needed
